Vesors is an İstanbul-based software development and technology consultancy. We help businesses build their digital infrastructure through corporate websites, e-commerce platforms, mobile applications and accounting software.

Through Vesors Digital, we bring software and digital marketing together: social media, advertising, SEO and competitor analysis support a connected approach to digital growth.

We also develop our own products, including Lokmate, an AI-powered nutrition tracking application.
